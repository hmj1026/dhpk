note: Fix the credential reader blocking a heredoc commit message whose body holds an apostrophe or a double quote, such as a `cat` heredoc inside `"$(...)"`. A heredoc declared in a double-quoted command substitution, including `<<-` and several on one line, is now skipped up to its delimiter line, as `command-inspect.sh` does. Unterminated quotes and substitutions outside heredoc bodies still block. An unquoted substitution, or one whose output may run as a command, keeps the stricter reading. `<<` inside arithmetic, `${...}` or `$[...]` is no longer read as a heredoc, so the lines after it are checked as commands (#1396).
